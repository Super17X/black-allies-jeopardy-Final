## Game setup

In advanced lobby settings, Question topics defaults to VA / PACT Act. Select 3–10 board categories. Each category draws five questions from its fifteen-question pool, using the existing browser rotation history. Select VA + MDE triage to mix content, or MDE triage for the legacy workbook bank. Final scenarios follow the selected topic.

Answers ignore case, spaces, punctuation and accents. Common short forms are accepted through explicit answer aliases. No broad fuzzy matching is used for medical or eligibility facts.

Every VA question includes a short explanation and official source URL. Location and timing rules are specific to the named presumption; burn pit and Gulf War airspace rules must not be interchanged. A listed diagnosis does not establish individual eligibility without the applicable service and evidence requirements.
